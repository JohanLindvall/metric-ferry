# Code of conduct

Metric Ferry welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project
discussions.

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Critique ideas and code rather than
  people, and make room for questions and disagreement.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, or post personal information without
  permission. Sexualized content and unwanted attention are not acceptable.
- Respect the time and boundaries of other contributors. Avoid spam and
  repeated demands for a response.

The repository maintainer, [Johan Lindvall](https://github.com/JohanLindvall),
handles conduct concerns and may remove content or restrict participation.
To request a private reporting channel, open an issue saying only that you want
to contact the maintainer privately about a conduct concern. Do not include
personal or sensitive details in that request. Reports will be considered in
context, with action proportionate to the behavior.
